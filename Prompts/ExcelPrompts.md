# Microsoft 365 Copilot in Excel
## Executive Prompt Library

> **Course:** AB-731 – Microsoft Certified: AI Transformation Leader
>
> **Application:** Microsoft Excel
>
> **Audience:** CEOs, CFOs, CIOs, COOs, CROs, Sales Directors, Finance Leaders, Executive Leadership Team
>
> **Purpose**
>
> This prompt library provides executive-grade Microsoft 365 Copilot prompts for analyzing enterprise data, creating dashboards, forecasting business performance, identifying risks, optimizing costs, and generating executive recommendations.

---

# Prompt Engineering Best Practices

For the best Copilot responses, include:

- Business Context
- Dataset Description
- Business Objective
- Desired Output
- Executive Audience
- Visualization Requirements

Example

Instead of asking:

> Analyze this data.

Ask:

> Analyze this enterprise sales dataset and prepare an Executive Quarterly Business Review including revenue, profitability, regional performance, growth trends, risks, opportunities, and executive recommendations with supporting charts.

# Best Practices

- Use clean, structured datasets with meaningful column names.
- Ask one focused business question at a time before expanding.
- Request charts, tables, and KPI cards where appropriate.
- Validate financial calculations before executive presentations.
- Combine Copilot insights with business expertise and organizational context.
- Use iterative prompting to refine results instead of relying on a single prompt.
