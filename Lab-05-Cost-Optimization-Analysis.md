# Lab Overview

Every executive leadership team is expected to improve profitability while maintaining business growth.

However, identifying unnecessary spending, budget overruns, operational inefficiencies, and optimization opportunities is often difficult because enterprise financial data is spread across multiple spreadsheets and business systems.

Microsoft 365 Copilot in Excel enables executives to analyze financial data conversationally, identify optimization opportunities, forecast savings, and generate strategic recommendations without requiring advanced Excel skills.

This demonstration focuses on how senior leadership can use AI to improve financial performance and support executive decision-making.

# Best Practices

- Use complete and validated financial datasets.
- Ensure consistent naming conventions across worksheets.
- Review AI-generated savings estimates before executive decisions.
- Combine Copilot insights with financial governance processes.
- Involve finance, procurement, and operations leaders when evaluating recommendations.
- Treat Copilot as a decision-support assistant rather than an autonomous financial advisor.

- Microsoft 365 Copilot enables executives to explore financial data using natural language instead of complex spreadsheet techniques.
