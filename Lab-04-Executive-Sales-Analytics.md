# Lab Overview

Every executive relies on data-driven decision making.

Sales leaders frequently ask questions such as:

- Which regions are performing well?
- Which products are driving revenue?
- Why has profitability declined?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue?
- What should we focus on next quarter?
- What actions should leadership take?

Traditionally, answering these questions requires:

- Pivot Tables
- Complex formulas
- Charts
- Dashboards
- Power Query
- Business Analysts

Microsoft 365 Copilot in Excel enables executives to ask these questions in natural language and receive meaningful business insights without requiring advanced Excel expertise.

This lab demonstrates how Microsoft 365 Copilot accelerates executive analytics, identifies trends, forecasts performance, and supports strategic decision-making.

# Best Practices

- Use clean, well-structured datasets with consistent column names.
- Ensure financial metrics are validated before executive meetings.
- Ask focused business questions instead of generic prompts.
- Refine prompts iteratively to uncover deeper insights.
- Validate forecasts against market conditions and business assumptions.
- Use Copilot as an analytical assistant, not a replacement for financial judgment.
