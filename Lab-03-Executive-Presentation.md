# Lab Overview

Senior leaders spend a significant amount of time creating executive presentations for:

- Board Meetings
- Investor Presentations
- Executive Committee Meetings
- Customer Briefings
- Strategy Workshops
- Quarterly Business Reviews
- Digital Transformation Reviews

Creating these presentations requires collecting information from multiple sources, structuring a compelling narrative, designing professional slides, and ensuring consistency in messaging.

Microsoft 365 Copilot in PowerPoint enables executives to transform business documents into polished, presentation-ready decks within minutes.

This lab demonstrates how Copilot accelerates executive storytelling while allowing leaders to focus on strategic discussions rather than slide creation.

# Best Practices

- Start from a well-structured Word document.
- Use Copilot to generate the first draft, then refine.
- Keep slides focused on business outcomes.
- Avoid excessive text.
- Use visuals to support—not replace—the narrative.
- Validate financial and strategic information before presenting.
- Tailor presentations to the intended audience (Board, Investors, Customers, Employees).
