> **Lab Type:** Executive Demonstration
>

>

>
> **Audience:** CXOs, Senior Leadership Team, Business Unit Heads, Directors, Vice Presidents

---

# Lab Overview

Executive leaders spend a considerable amount of time reading lengthy business reports before making strategic decisions.

These reports often include:

- Financial Reports
- Sales Performance Reports
- Customer Feedback
- Operational Updates
- Risk Assessments
- Project Status Reports
- Market Analysis
- Compliance Reports

Reviewing multiple reports manually consumes valuable executive time.

Microsoft 365 Copilot in Word enables leaders to transform lengthy documents into concise, actionable executive summaries within minutes while maintaining context and traceability.

# Demonstration 1
## Executive Summary

### Business Context

The CEO does not have time to read the complete report before the board meeting.

The objective is to produce a concise executive summary.

---

### Prompt

```text
Create an Executive Summary for the Board of Directors based on this report.

Include:

• Overall business performance

• Financial highlights

• Operational performance

• Customer satisfaction

• Major achievements

• Strategic concerns

Limit the summary to one page.

Use a professional executive tone.
```

---

## Expected Output

Copilot generates

- One-page executive summary
- Revenue overview
- Profit trends
- Manufacturing highlights
- Customer trends
- Business challenges
- Future priorities

---

## Leadership Discussion

Ask participants

> If you were the CEO, would this one-page summary be sufficient to understand the company's current position?

# Demonstration 3
## Business Risk Assessment

### Business Context

Every Board Meeting begins with enterprise risk discussion.

Instead of manually reviewing the Risk Register, Copilot can identify the most critical risks.

---

### Prompt

```text
Identify the five most critical business risks mentioned in this report.

For each risk provide

• Business Impact

• Probability

• Department

• Recommended Mitigation

Present the output as a table.
```

---

## Expected Output

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Recommendation |
|------|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Supply Chain Delay | High | High | Diversify suppliers |
| Rising Material Cost | High | Medium | Strategic procurement |
| Cybersecurity | Very High | Medium | Accelerate Zero Trust |
| Talent Attrition | Medium | High | Leadership development |
| Regulatory Compliance | High | Low | Quarterly audits |

---

## Leadership Discussion

Ask

> Which of these risks requires immediate executive attention?

# Key Takeaways

- Microsoft 365 Copilot enables executives to consume large volumes of business information quickly.
- Natural language prompts replace complex manual analysis for many executive review tasks.
- AI-generated summaries accelerate preparation for board meetings while keeping users grounded in enterprise content.
- Executives remain accountable for validating insights, applying business judgment, and making final decisions.
- Effective prompting improves the quality, clarity, and actionability of Copilot outputs.
